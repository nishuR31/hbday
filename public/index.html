Privacy Terms & Anti-Abuse Disclaimer

1. Privacy Policy & Data Protection

HBday values your privacy and personal trust. Contact names, dates of birth, and email addresses provided to this service are used exclusively for scheduling and dispatching birthday celebration greetings. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose personal data to third parties, ad networks, or data brokers.

2. "Do Not Misuse" & Anti-Abuse Clause

Strict Zero-Tolerance Anti-Spam Policy: This service is strictly intended for celebratory greetings to personal friends, family, and colleagues. You strictly agree NOT to:

  • Submit email addresses without prior consent of the recipient.
  • Send unsolicited commercial bulk email (spam), marketing solicitations, or deceptive content.
  • Include defamatory, offensive, harassing, obscene, or threatening language in celebration messages.
  • Attempt to circumvent daily addition quotas (5 daily / 15 weekly) or flood the queue via automated bots or scripts.

Any violation of this anti-abuse policy will result in immediate termination of the associated passkey, IP blacklisting, and permanent purging of stored records.

3. Passkey Identity Model

Your account-less passkey is stored locally on your device. You are responsible for preserving your passkey if you wish to manage your birthday entries across multiple browsers or devices.
